ABOUT ME

I don't just look at the numbers. I look for the story behind them.

I'm a Business & Data Analyst who focuses on turning business data into clear insights that support better decisions. I combine business thinking with practical analytical skills across Microsoft Excel, SQL, Python, Data Visualization, Statistics and Data Preparation.

Through hands-on analytical projects, I have worked with transactional and business datasets to investigate revenue performance, profitability, customer and product behaviour, operational performance and business opportunities. I am particularly interested in using data to understand what is happening, why it is happening, and what a business can do next.

My approach goes beyond simply reporting numbers. I focus on understanding the business problem, assessing data quality, identifying meaningful patterns, translating findings into business insights, and communicating practical recommendations to decision-makers.

I believe good analysis should not leave people staring at a spreadsheet wondering what to do next. It should make decisions clearer.
