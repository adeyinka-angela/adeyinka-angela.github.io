ABOUT ME

I don't just look at the numbers. I look for the story behind them.

I'm a Business Analyst who enjoys turning information into something people can actually understand and use.

I’m interested in the questions behind the numbers:
What happened? Why did it happen? What does it mean for the business? And, most importantly, what should happen next?

My approach combines analytical thinking, business understanding and clear communication. I use tools such as Excel, SQL, data visualization, statistics and Python to explore problems, identify patterns and communicate insights.

I believe good analysis should not leave people staring at a spreadsheet wondering what to do next. It should make decisions clearer.
