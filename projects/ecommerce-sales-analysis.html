01 · PROJECT OVERVIEW

From a business question to a decision.

This end-to-end business analytics case study investigates the performance of PalmBridge Retail Co., an online retailer generating more than $1.6M in sales across a three-year period.

The analysis moves from data preparation and cleaning through Excel analysis, SQL validation and Tableau visualization to identify what was driving revenue, where profitability was being lost and what the business should do next.
