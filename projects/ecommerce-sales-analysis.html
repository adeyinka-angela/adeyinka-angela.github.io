01 · PROJECT OVERVIEW

From a business question to a decision.

This end-to-end business analytics case study investigates the performance of PalmBridge Retail Co., an online retailer generating more than $1.6M in sales across a three-year period.

The analysis moves from data preparation and cleaning through Excel analysis, SQL validation and Tableau visualization to identify what was driving revenue, where profitability was being lost and what the business should do next.

02 · BUSINESS PROBLEM

Strong sales. Weak profitability.

THE CENTRAL BUSINESS PROBLEM

PalmBridge Retail Co. was generating more than $1.6 million in sales across three years, yet total profit was just -$121.

Sales grew from $529,568 in 2022 to $566,313 in 2024, while profit declined from +$6,165 to -$1,724.

The business was growing its revenue while moving in the opposite direction financially.

The analysis therefore focused on understanding what was driving the profitability problem and where management could intervene.

ANALYSIS OBJECTIVES

  • Identify which product categories and sub-categories were profitable versus loss-making.
  • Quantify the relationship between discounting behaviour and profitability outcomes.
  • Determine whether losses were concentrated in specific regions or customer segments.
  • Analyse the year-over-year trend in revenue, profit and margin from 2022 to 2024.
  • Deliver five specific, prioritised and actionable recommendations to restore profitability.
